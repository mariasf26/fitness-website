Our Workouts

Squats

Squats strengthen your legs and improve your lower body strength.

Recommended: 3 × 15 repetitions

Push-ups

Push-ups work your chest, shoulders and arms.

Recommended: 3 × 10 repetitions

Lunges

Lunges are excellent for strengthening the legs and improving balance.

Recommended: 3 × 10 each leg

Plank

The plank helps strengthen your abdominal and core muscles.

Recommended: 30 seconds × 3

Jumping Jacks

Jumping jacks are a simple exercise that increases your heart rate.

Recommended: 30 seconds × 3

Sit-ups

Sit-ups help strengthen your abdominal muscles.

Recommended: 3 × 15 repetitions
